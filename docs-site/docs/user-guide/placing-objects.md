title: Placing Objects
---

- Open "Event Objects" and click an item to activate placement. Shift-click to enable batch placement.
- Move the cursor to preview location; click the map to place.
- Rotation: use , and . to rotate in 45° steps for objects that support snapping. Hold for continuous rotation where applicable.
- Flip (if available): toggle orientation per object type.
- Cancel: click the active item again to exit placement; Esc also cancels.
