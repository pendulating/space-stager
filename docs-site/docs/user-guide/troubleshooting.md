title: Troubleshooting
---

- Data doesn’t load
  - Check your network and try zooming in. Some NYC OpenData endpoints rate‑limit; wait and retry.
- Drawing tools say “initializing”
  - Click Retry in the tools panel or switch basemap and back to force rebind.
- PNG/PDF export cuts off elements
  - Keep the tab visible while exporting; avoid switching tabs during render.
