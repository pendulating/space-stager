This example demonstrates a compact “streetery” layout in a high-footfall area. It balances vendor placement with clear pedestrian aisles and emergency access.

## Design notes

- Maintain a minimum 8–10 ft pedestrian aisle through the corridor.
- Cluster utilities and clearance zones to reduce clutter.
- Use signage to separate service and public areas.
